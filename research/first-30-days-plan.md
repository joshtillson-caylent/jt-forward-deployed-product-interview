# First 30 Days: Sequence and Rationale

Backs slide 7 of the kickoff deck. Built around the team's close calendar, because the time of month decides how much a finance team can give. Kickoff is Tue, Sep 29, the day before Q3 quarter-end. That makes week 1 the team's busiest week of the quarter.

All dates below are `hypothesis — validate`. We're assuming close runs BD1–BD7 (Oct 1–9) and that Q3 board prep lands somewhere in weeks 3–4. David confirms both at kickoff, before we book anything.

## Day 0 (Tue, Sep 29): Kick off

- Confirm the close calendar, the board date, and any other blackout windows.
- Name champions. Are the two analysts on the call the right ones, and is anyone else a better fit?
- Book the week-1 interviews and the week-2 workshop into windows David has cleared.
- Agree ways of working: check-in cadence, channel, and who makes the call on what the team tries.
- Request artifacts and access: last variance pack, report templates, last board narrative, data-handling rules, and Claude Enterprise usage data.
- **Team time:** 60 min (David + 2 analysts).
- **Why first:** everything later depends on knowing when the team can't be interrupted.

## Week 1 (Oct 1–9): Listen

- 30-min 1:1 interviews, scheduled around close, not during its peak.
- Shadow one real close task. Watch rather than interrupt.
- Collect templates and prior packs so the workshop can run on real material.
- Find any team at Meridian already using Claude well, using the usage data and David's leads, and interview them. If adoption is low org-wide, that tells us something about enablement. If it isn't, those people are internal proof and possible champions.
- Review what the orientation covered, and whether any toolkit came with it.
- **Team time:** ~30 min per person.
- **Why now:** close is the worst time to ask for workshops and the best time to see the real work.

## Week 2 (Oct 12–16): Discover

- 90-min discovery workshop after close (design in `discovery/discovery-session-design.md`).
- Walk through a real recent task, sort judgment from assembly, and run a live first pass on Q3 numbers.
- Pick one workflow **with David**, scored on the lighthouse criteria: frequent, standard shape, already reviewed, low blast radius.
- Give David the board-ready sentence for the sponsor.
- **Team time:** 90 min.

## Week 3 (Oct 19–23): Build and iterate with champions

- Build the first workflow as a shared Claude Project, a Skill, and a prompt kit. Use Claude for Excel if the work lives in spreadsheets.
- Demo to champions twice and iterate on their feedback between demos.
- If board prep lands here, demos shrink to 20 minutes and we test on September data.
- **Team time:** 2 × 20-min demos.

## Week 4 (Oct 26–30): Adopt and read out

- Champions run the workflow on real work, with draft-then-verify on every output.
- Measure draft keep-rate (Anthropic's pilot metric: how often a draft is kept without a meaningful rewrite), hours saved per cycle, and unprompted use.
- Hold a 30-min readout with David: evidence in the sponsor's terms, plus a backlog of next workflows.
- **Team time:** 30 min.

## How it runs: agile, owned by the team

- Build → demo → feedback → iterate, in short loops with the champions.
- Champions own the workflow by day 30. They present it to the rest of the team, not us.
- The next workflow comes from the week-2 backlog and follows the same loop.

## After day 30 (not in scope yet)

- Roll out to the remaining analysts, led by the champions.
- Once a workflow is proven and owned, the Tier 2 conversation starts: connect it to the data, then automate it up to the review step (slide 8).
