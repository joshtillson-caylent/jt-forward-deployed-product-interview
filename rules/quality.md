# Product Artifact Quality Standards

## Before declaring a product artifact complete:

- [ ] All requirements are testable and unambiguous
- [ ] Success criteria are measurable with identified data sources
- [ ] Scope boundaries are explicit (in-scope and out-of-scope stated)
- [ ] Acceptance criteria are written from the user's perspective
- [ ] Edge cases and error states are addressed
- [ ] Dependencies are identified and stakeholders are named
- [ ] Trade-offs and alternatives considered are documented

## User story quality:

- Every user story follows the format: "As a [persona], I want [action] so that [outcome]"
- Acceptance criteria use Given/When/Then or clear bullet format
- Stories are small enough to complete in a single sprint
- Stories are independent and deliverable on their own

## Writing standards:

- Write for the audience (engineers need specifics, execs need outcomes)
- Remove jargon unless the audience expects it
- Favor concrete examples over abstract descriptions
- Do not leave placeholder text or TBD sections in final artifacts
