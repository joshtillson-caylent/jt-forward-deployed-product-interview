# Value Charter — Meridian Capital Services

## Engagement intent

Turn a board-level "AI-enabled operations" mandate — currently stalled at near-zero adoption despite real license spend — into a working, trusted pattern inside FP&A that David can point to and the PE sponsor can recognize as progress. Not a chatbot rollout; a workflow-embedded pilot that preserves analyst judgment as the final check.

## Business outcomes this ladders to

- The PE sponsor's value-creation thesis line item ("AI-enabled operations") gets real evidence behind it, not just license-purchase evidence
- David has language and a track record to bring to the board that isn't "we bought the tool"
- FP&A gets time back on mechanical work (reconciliation, formatting, first-draft narrative) without giving up control of judgment calls

## Value levers

| Lever | How this engagement moves it |
|---|---|
| Time-to-close | Reduce manual reconciliation/formatting time in variance write-ups and board narrative drafting |
| Trust in the mandate | Give David a concrete, demonstrable win instead of an abstract initiative |
| Risk reduction | Keep a human final check on anything numbers- or judgment-bearing — build trust by design, not by asking for it |
| Scalability | Whatever works for the first 1-2 analysts should generalize to the remaining 6 without a second from-scratch discovery |

## Operating principles

- **Problem first, not tech first** — every recommendation traces to a specific pain in `pain-points.md`, not a capability we want to show off.
- **Evidence over assumption** — everything not cited to the brief is labeled `hypothesis — validate` until the kickoff call confirms or corrects it.
- **Judgment stays with the analyst** — the pitch is draft-then-verify, never autonomous numbers or narrative.
- **David needs to succeed, not just the pilot** — see `deliverables/david-okafor-first-two-weeks.md`.

## Success criteria (draft — confirm with David)

- A lighthouse use case selected and agreed with David by the end of week 2
- A small guided pilot running with 1-2 analysts by week 4
- A checkpoint readout ready for the PE sponsor that describes real time saved or risk reduced, not license utilization

This charter is the scoring lens for any later Assess-phase work (use-case prioritization, build sequence) — if scoring ever feels wrong, revisit this document, not the rubric.
