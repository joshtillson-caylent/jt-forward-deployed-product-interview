# Pain Point Inventory — Meridian FP&A

Evidence ledger: see `case-file/00-engagement-brief.md`. Everything below is either cited to the brief or labeled `hypothesis — validate`; nothing here has been confirmed in a live conversation yet.

## Named in the brief (cited)

1. **Adoption is near zero, 6 months post-license-purchase.** [Source: scenario-brief.pdf]
2. **The team frames their work as immune to AI**: *"I just don't see how it applies to what we do. Our work is numbers-based and requires judgment."* [Source: scenario-brief.pdf]
3. **The workload leaves little slack** — monthly close, budget-vs-actuals reconciliation, variance analysis, management reporting, and the quarterly board narrative are continuous, not occasional. [Source: scenario-brief.pdf]
4. **David has tried prompting himself, with mixed results**, and doesn't have the bandwidth to turn that into a program on his own. [Source: scenario-brief.pdf]
5. **The 1-hour orientation didn't translate into use.** [Source: scenario-brief.pdf] — `hypothesis — validate`: *why* it didn't (generic training vs. workflow-specific need) is our inference, not stated in the brief.

## Hypothesized (validate at kickoff)

6. **`hypothesis — validate`:** Variance write-ups and board-narrative drafting involve substantial manual reconciliation and formatting labor that analysts don't name as "a problem" because it's simply understood to be the job.
7. **`hypothesis — validate`:** The orientation presented AI as a general capability ("here's a chatbot") rather than anchored to a specific task in their actual workflow — which is why it didn't stick.
8. **`hypothesis — validate`:** Analysts are wary that an AI-drafted number or narrative could misrepresent their judgment if a reviewer or the board took it at face value — a trust/liability concern, not a competence one.
9. **`hypothesis — validate`:** David's two mixed-result prompt attempts were likely generic ("summarize this" / "help me write this") rather than grounded in Meridian's actual data or templates — worth confirming directly, since it shapes whether the fix is "better prompting" or "a different kind of tool support."
10. **`hypothesis — validate`:** The PE sponsor's definition of "AI-enabled operations" success is still undefined even internally — meaning part of this engagement's value is helping *define* the success criteria, not just hit a pre-set one.
