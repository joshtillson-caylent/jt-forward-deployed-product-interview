# Discovery Questions — Kickoff Call (David + 2 analysts)

Organized by what each question is actually trying to surface. Aim to spend most of the call on Core/Workflow — that's where the invisible pain lives (see `discovery-session-design.md` for the full session design this question bank supports).

## Opening (rapport, framing — 5 min)

1. Before we get into specifics — what does success look like to you personally at the end of this, separate from whatever the PE sponsor is measuring?
2. What's the honest version of how the last 6 months have gone with the Claude licenses, from where you sit?

## Context (David's situation specifically)

3. Walk me through what the orientation actually covered — was there anything FP&A-specific in it, or was it general?
4. You mentioned trying a few prompts yourself — what were you trying to do, and where did it fall short?
5. What does the PE sponsor actually see or hear about this initiative today? What would a good update to them look like?

## Core / workflow (where the real evidence lives — spend the most time here)

6. Walk me through the last variance write-up you did, start to finish, minute by minute. (This is the single most important question — see session design.)
7. Of everything in that walkthrough, which parts are judgment calls only you can make, and which parts are just... assembling and formatting?
8. How much of the monthly close is genuinely novel analysis versus the same reconciliation pattern every month?
9. When you write the board narrative, what are you actually starting from — last quarter's version, a template, a blank page?
10. Is there a step in this process you'd trust an intern with, if you had the headcount? Why or why not?
11. Where do numbers get double-checked today, and by whom? What would make you trust a first draft you didn't write yourself?

## Solution exploration (only after core is well-covered)

12. If a tool could produce a first-pass draft of one part of this, which part would actually save you time versus just moving the work somewhere else?
13. What would make you *not* trust an AI-drafted variance explanation, even if the numbers were right?
14. Has anyone on the team used Claude for anything, even informally, outside the mandatory orientation?

## Closing

15. What's the one thing that, if we get it right in the first two weeks, tells you this is actually going somewhere?
16. Who else on the team should we talk to, and is there anyone who'd push back hard on this if we're not careful?

## Added for the kickoff (2026-09-28): calendar, org context, orientation

All `hypothesis — validate`. These were added after the deck was reframed around the close calendar and the org-wide adoption question.

17. When does close actually run: which business days, and which are the worst? When is the Q3 board meeting, and when does narrative prep start? (Nothing gets booked until this is answered.)
18. Is near-zero adoption an FP&A thing, or is it the same across Meridian? Can we see the Claude Enterprise usage data?
19. Is any team at Meridian using Claude well today? Could we talk to them?
20. What did the orientation cover: a general tour, example prompts, or anything FP&A-specific? Did anyone leave it with a toolkit, prompts, or a workflow they could reuse?
21. What data can go into Claude Enterprise today, and who sets that rule?

## Explicitly not asking

We're deliberately not opening with "what's your biggest pain point" or "how do you feel about AI" — per `discovery-notes.md`, those questions get "nothing" from a team that's never had a before/after contrast to notice.
