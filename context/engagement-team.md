# Engagement Context — Meridian Capital Services

## Mission

Meridian's PE sponsor made "AI-enabled operations" an explicit part of the value-creation thesis when it acquired Meridian 11 months ago. The FP&A team bought Claude enterprise licenses org-wide 6 months ago, ran a 1-hour mandatory orientation, and adoption since has been near zero — not from resistance, but because the team can't see where a chat tool fits work that's "numbers-based and requires judgment." Caylent's engagement: turn that license spend into actual adoption, starting with FP&A, in a way that preserves analyst judgment rather than trying to route around it.
