# Is "AI Isn't Relevant to Our Work" a Legitimate Read, or a Misread?

## The position

**It's a misread of the tool, not a misread of the team.** The team is accurately describing why *generic chat prompting* doesn't fit judgment-heavy, numbers-based work — and then, understandably, generalizing that into "AI doesn't apply here." Those are two different claims, and only the first one is true.

## The argument

1. **The orientation taught a tool, not a task.** A 1-hour mandatory session on Claude, with no FP&A-specific worked example, teaches "here is a chatbot" — not "here is where this touches your Tuesday." Nobody walked out of that session having seen their own variance write-up done differently. [Source: scenario-brief.pdf]
2. **David's own mixed-result attempts are evidence for this, not against it.** If his prompts were general ("help me write this," "summarize this") rather than grounded in Meridian's actual numbers and templates, mixed results are exactly what you'd expect — and exactly what doesn't generalize into "this doesn't work for us." `hypothesis — validate` at kickoff.
3. **"Numbers-based and requires judgment" is a real constraint, not a wall.** It rules out *autonomous* AI output on anything that reaches a reviewer or the board. It does not rule out AI *drafting* something a human still checks — which is the entire model this engagement proposes.

## What this changes about the approach

- **The pitch can't be a capability demo.** It has to open on one of the team's own recent tasks, live (see `discovery/discovery-session-design.md`).
- **The sequencing can't lead with breadth.** One lighthouse use case, chosen with David, beats a menu of possibilities — a team this busy will not self-serve exploration.
- **The trust model has to be explicit from day one.** Draft-then-verify, judgment stays with the analyst, every time — stated up front, not discovered as a workaround after skepticism.
- **Success has to be defined in the team's terms**, not "adoption %" — time saved on a specific task, or a board narrative David still fully stands behind, not a usage dashboard.

If any of this turns out wrong at the kickoff call — if the team genuinely can't identify a task where a first draft would help — that's a real signal to revisit the whole approach, not a reason to push the same pitch harder.
