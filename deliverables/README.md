# Deliverables

The point of view — the working strategy documents that back the deck, one per required topic from `case-file/scenario-brief.pdf`. This is where the thinking happens in full; `presentations/` is where it gets compressed to 5-8 slides.

| File | Deck topic it answers |
|------|------------------------|
| `ai-relevance-position.md` | Is "AI isn't relevant to our work" a legitimate read or a misread — and what that changes about the approach |
| `david-okafor-first-two-weeks.md` | What David specifically needs from Josh in the first two weeks |
| `first-30-days-plan.md` | The first 30 days — what, in what order, and why |
| `anthropic-finance-evidence.md` | Supporting evidence: how finance teams use Claude, Anthropic's terms, and the partner tiers (slides 4 and 8) |

(The fourth required topic — the discovery motion / session design — lives in `discovery/discovery-session-design.md` instead, since it's a discovery artifact, not a strategy position.)
